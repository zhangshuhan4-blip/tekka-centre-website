Five points of significance

The importance of Tekka Centre

From its historic roots to its role today, each part of Tekka tells a story about Singapore.

Point 01 · Heritage01

Heritage Anchor of Little India

  • Origins: Established in 1915 as the “Kandang Kerbau” (Buffalo Enclosure) Market.
  • Social anchor: For generations, the market has been an important gathering place in the Little India historic district.
Point 03 · Function03

An Unrivalled Ingredient Hub

  • For cooks: The market serves home cooks and food professionals looking for ingredients beyond the usual supermarket range.
  • Specialties: Shoppers can find South Asian vegetables, fresh meat and freshly prepared coconut milk (santan).
Point 02 · Core identity02

A Multicultural Microcosm

Tekka Centre reflects Singapore’s diversity through everyday interactions between stallholders and customers.

  • Side-by-side harmony: Chinese, Malay and Indian communities share the same marketplace.
  • Linguistic fluidity: Vendors and customers may communicate in English, Hokkien, Tamil, Malay and other languages, helping people from different backgrounds connect.
Point 04 · Tourism04

A Culinary Tourism Landmark

  • Dual experience: Visitors can explore the wet market and enjoy food at the hawker centre within the same complex.
  • Sensory space: The sights, sounds, aromas and ingredients offer visitors a close-up look at Singapore’s food culture.
Point 05 · Legacy05

A Symbol of Community Resilience

  • Name conflict: The centre was renamed “Zhujiao Centre” in 1982, a name that was not widely embraced by the public.
  • Reclaimed identity: The name “Tekka Centre” was restored in 2000, reflecting the community’s attachment to the familiar name and its heritage.
